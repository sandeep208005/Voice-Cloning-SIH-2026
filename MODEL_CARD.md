# DeepShield AI — Forensic Model Cards

## Model Overview

This document specifies the provenance, training methodologies, feature extraction inputs, evaluation protocols, and operational limitations of the forensic models deployed within DeepShield AI.

---

## 1. DeepShield-AcousticForensics-v1

- **Model Type**: Acoustic Spectral Anomaly & Vocoder Fingerprint Classifier
- **Version**: 1.2.0
- **Primary Modality**: Single-channel audio (WAV, MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG)
- **Sample Rate**: Standardized to 22,050 Hz Mono
- **Inference Runtime**: Python 3.14 / Librosa / SciPy Signal Processing

### Inputs
- Raw audio waveform array $y \in \mathbb{R}^N$
- Extracted features:
  - 20 Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs)
  - Spectral Centroid & Spectral Rolloff (85% energy)
  - Spectral Flatness
  - Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR)
  - Harmonic-to-Percussive Energy Ratio (HPSS)
  - Micro-pitch Jitter ($F_0$ perturbation via probabilistic YIN algorithm)
  - High-frequency Vocoder Cutoff Ratio ($> 7,500\text{ Hz}$)

### Intended Use
Detection of synthetic speech generated by neural text-to-speech (TTS) vocoders (HiFi-GAN, WaveGlow, MelGAN) and commercial cloning APIs (ElevenLabs, VALL-E, Bark).

### Out-of-Scope & Limitations
- Audio with severe background noise or low bitrate compression ($< 32\text{ kbps}$) can produce spectral rolloff artifacts mimicking vocoders.
- Samples shorter than 0.8 seconds lack sufficient vocal cycles to reliably measure micro-pitch perturbation.

## 2. DeepShield-VisualForensics-v1

- **Model Type**: Spatial Quantization & 2D-FFT Frequency Domain Detector
- **Version**: 1.2.0
- **Primary Modality**: Digital Images (JPG, PNG, WEBP)
- **Inference Runtime**: Python 3.14 / OpenCV 5 / NumPy / PIL

### Inputs
- BGR image tensor $I \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times 3}$
- Extracted features:
  - Facial bounding coordinates and landmark geometry
  - Regional Error Level Analysis (ELA) compression discrepancy between facial zone and background
  - 2D-FFT radial magnitude spectrum and azimuthal high-frequency spike ratio
  - Sobel boundary gradient standard deviation along facial perimeter
  - Laplacian variance sharpness metric

### Intended Use
Identification of image synthesis from diffusion models (Stable Diffusion, Midjourney) and composite face-swapping software (DeepFaceLab, FaceFusion, Roop).

### Limitations
- Images that have undergone multiple successive heavy JPEG re-compressions across social media platforms may mask original boundary feathering.
- Extreme low resolution ($< 128 \times 128$) limits high-frequency spike detection.

## 3. DeepShield-TemporalForensics-v1

- **Model Type**: Multi-Frame Spatial-Temporal Jitter & Consistency Aggregator
- **Version**: 1.2.0
- **Primary Modality**: Video Streams (MP4, WEBM, MOV)
- **Inference Runtime**: Python 3.14 / OpenCV Video Engine

### Inputs
- Uniform sequence of $K$ frames ($12 \le K \le 32$) across video duration
- Extracted features:
  - Per-frame spatial and frequency anomaly scores
  - Inter-frame Euclidean center displacement trajectory variance
  - Bounding box scale fluctuation standard deviation
  - Farnebäck optical flow residual velocity

### Intended Use
Detection of deepfake videos where individual frames exhibit independent facial bounding flicker or temporal warping.

### Limitations
- Handheld shaky camera movements can elevate overall motion velocity. DeepShield mitigates this by measuring face displacement relative to background optical flow.

## 4. Ethical & Legal Considerations

1. **Probabilistic Nature**: Model outputs are calibrated probabilities, not deterministic proof of criminality.
2. **False Positives**: Heavily filtered, stylized, or low-quality authentic media may trigger medium-risk alerts.
3. **Privacy**: Uploaded biometrics are processed in memory and sanitized immediately upon deletion request. DeepShield never aggregates user media to train public models without consent.
